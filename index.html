📚 How do these two rankings differ?

📜 The Old Way — 1997

Early search engines counted how many times your search word appeared in a page. This was easy to spam — just repeat the word over and over to rank first.

  • Simple word-frequency count
  • Spam pages like pizza-pizza-pizza.com win
  • Ignores link structure of the web

🏄 The Surfer Way — 1998

Imagine a person randomly clicking links, occasionally teleporting to a random page. Pages visited often are popular & trustworthy. This became the seed of PageRank.

  • Popularity = how often surfer lands here
  • Spam pages have no inbound links → low score
  • Well-connected hubs naturally rise to the top
